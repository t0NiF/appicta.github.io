What is stored in your browser

If you switch the language (EN/DE), your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name "appicta-lang" so the site opens in the same language next time. It never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time:

Fonts and hosting

The pages load the fonts Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com). When a page loads, your browser therefore sends technical data such as your IP address to Google. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may also log technical access data. Please see the privacy statements of Google and GitHub for details.
